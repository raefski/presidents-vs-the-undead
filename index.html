PRESIDENTS vs. THE UNDEAD

Nine armies of history have dug into Colonial Williamsburg. Take back all nine buildings.

iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then play from the icon — it runs full screen with no address bar.

A parody. No actual presidents were consulted.

HOW TO PLAY

Move

WASD or the arrow keys, or a gamepad stick. That is the entire control scheme.

Move

Hold your thumb anywhere on the screen and drag. A stick appears where you touch, so you never have to look for it. Let go to stop.

Attack

You never attack manually. Every weapon fires on its own timer. Your job is where you stand.

Spend XP

XP is money. Press Esc to open the War Room and buy exactly the upgrade you want — no random cards.

Spend XP

XP is money. Tap UPGRADE (bottom right) to open the War Room and buy exactly the upgrade you want — no random cards. It glows when you can afford something.

Your Arsenal

Max your primary to unlock your secondary. Max both to unlock your fusion — which the last strongpoints genuinely require.

Strongpoints

Nine buildings, nine bosses, nine garrisons. They hold position. Walk into one under-levelled and you will die. Grind first.

Staff

Gold hires an assistant who fights beside you. Save up. They are not good, but they are keen.

Mini-bosses

Something large comes hunting roughly every eighty seconds. It pays well and it does not hold still.

Keys

Esc upgrade · P pause · M mute · F fullscreen
(Tab also opens the War Room — useful in fullscreen, where the browser keeps Esc for itself.)

On a phone

Hold it sideways. The II and speaker buttons in the top right pause and mute. Turning the phone upright pauses the game for you.
